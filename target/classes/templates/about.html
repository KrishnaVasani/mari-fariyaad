About Mari-Fariyaad Estate Portal

Mari-Fariyaad (મારી-ફરિયાદ) is the centralized digital complaint resolution and estate maintenance portal for Gujarat Vidyapith. The portal streamlines infrastructure maintenance across all University Departments, Hostels, Estate Areas, and Campus Facilities.

Whether you are a student, faculty member, administrative staff, hostel resident, or campus visitor, Mari-Fariyaad provides a transparent, accountable, and time-bound platform to report civil, electrical, sanitation, plumbing, and IT maintenance issues.

Our Core Objectives
  • 100% Digital Tracking: Real-time status updates from ticket generation to final resolution.
  • Accountability & Speed: SLA-driven task allocation to authorized estate technicians.
  • Multi-Language Accessibility: Native support for Gujarati, English, and Hindi.
  • Campus-Wide Scope: Dedicated coverage for Hostels, Departments, and Estate Maintenance.

Complaint Resolution Lifecycle

1
1. Registration
User files a complaint with mandatory Email and location details.
2
2. Triage & Allocation

3
3. On-Site Action

4
4. Verification & Closure
Ticket status updated to Resolved with email confirmation to user.
